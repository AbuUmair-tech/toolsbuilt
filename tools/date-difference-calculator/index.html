How Many Days Are Between Two Dates?

To calculate how many days are between two dates, subtract the start date from the end date. Our date difference tool calculates the total elapsed calendar days, working business days (Monday through Friday), weeks, months, and full year spans automatically.

What is the Difference Between Business Days and Calendar Days?

Calendar days include every day of the week (Monday through Sunday), totalling 7 days per week. Business days measure working days strictly from Monday through Friday, excluding Saturday and Sunday weekend days.

For project management and legal contracts, distinguishing between 30 calendar days and 30 business days is crucial, as 30 business days represent approximately 6 full calendar weeks.

How Do You Calculate Inclusive vs Exclusive Date Ranges?

In standard exclusive date math, Monday to Tuesday is 1 day. However, in inclusive calculations (such as hotel stays or vacation days), both Monday and Tuesday are counted, yielding 2 full days. Checking the inclusive option adds 1 day to account for the starting boundary.

Leap Year Calculation Accuracy

Our calculation algorithm processes dates at local midnight (`00:00:00`), accounting for February 29th in leap years automatically to prevent off-by-one errors across leap cycles.
