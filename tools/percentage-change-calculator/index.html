Key Facts & Formula Reference

Formula Summary

  • Percent Change: ((New - Old) / |Old|) × 100
  • Percent Increase: Base + (Base × Rate)
  • Percent Decrease: Base - (Base × Rate)
  • Proportion Ratio: (Part / Total) × 100

How Do You Calculate Percentage Change Between Two Numbers?

To calculate percentage change between two numbers, subtract the old value from the new value, divide the difference by the absolute value of the old value, and multiply by 100. A positive answer indicates a percentage increase, while a negative answer indicates a percentage decrease.

How Do You Increase or Decrease a Number by a Percentage?

To increase or decrease a number by a percentage, calculate the percentage portion (multiply base number by percent rate divided by 100) and then add (for increases) or subtract (for decreases) that portion from the starting number.

Understanding the Reversibility Trap

A +50% increase followed by a -50% decrease does not return you to your original starting number. For example, $100 + 50% = $150. Decreasing $150 by 50% removes $75, resulting in $75.
